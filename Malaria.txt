Malaria – fever at specific intervals (often alternate days), chills, sweating
Dengue – high continuous fever, severe body pain, headache behind eyes, rash
Typhoid – long-lasting fever, weakness, abdominal pain, loss of appetite
Common cold – runny nose, sneezing, mild fever, sore throat
Influenza (flu) – sudden high fever, body aches, fatigue, dry cough
COVID-19 – fever, dry cough, loss of smell/taste, breathing difficulty
Tuberculosis – chronic cough >2 weeks, weight loss, night sweats, mild fever
Pneumonia – fever, chest pain, cough with phlegm, breathing difficulty
Bronchitis – persistent cough with mucus, chest discomfort, mild fever
Asthma – wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, worse at night
Gastroenteritis – vomiting, diarrhea, stomach cramps, mild fever
Food poisoning – sudden vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain after eating
Appendicitis – pain starting near navel → right lower abdomen, nausea, fever
Kidney stone – severe side/back pain, blood in urine, nausea
Urinary tract infection – burning urination, frequent urge, lower abdominal pain
Diabetes – excessive thirst, frequent urination, weight loss, fatigue
Hypertension – often no symptoms, sometimes headache or dizziness
Migraine – one-sided throbbing headache, nausea, light sensitivity
Meningitis – high fever, neck stiffness, confusion, headache
Chickenpox – itchy fluid-filled rash, fever, fatigue
Measles – fever, cough, runny nose, red rash starting on face
Hepatitis A – jaundice, fatigue, abdominal pain, nausea
Jaundice (general) – yellow skin/eyes, dark urine, pale stool
Anemia – fatigue, pale skin, dizziness, shortness of breath
Hypothyroidism – weight gain, cold intolerance, fatigue, dry skin
Hyperthyroidism – weight loss, sweating, fast heartbeat, anxiety
Allergic rhinitis – sneezing, runny nose, itchy eyes, seasonal pattern
Sinusitis – facial pain, nasal blockage, headache, thick nasal discharge
GERD (acid reflux) – burning chest pain after meals, sour taste, cough at night
Peptic ulcer – stomach pain when empty, relief after eating, bloating
